Trigger Phrases

When this skill activates

Claude consults this skill whenever any of the following phrases appear, or when a raw reference list is pasted alongside a formatting request.

APA 7'ye göre düzenle kaynakçayı düzelt format references APA formatına çevir Türkçe başlıkları çevir fix bibliography reference list + paste

Core APA 7 Rules

  • No publication place for books — only publisher name (Sage. not New York: Sage.)
  • Publisher name — shortened official form (Sage not Sage Publications)
  • DOI format — always https://doi.org/…; replace http://dx.doi.org/ and bare DOIs
  • Italics — journal name + volume number; book titles; edited book titles
  • Author count — list all up to 20; for 21+ list first 19, then …, then last author
  • "Retrieved from" — remove for stable URLs/DOIs; keep only for content that changes over time
  • Edition info — (3rd ed.) after title, before period, before publisher

Journal Articles

Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article. *Journal Name*, *vol*(issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxx
  • Article title: sentence case, no italics
  • Journal name: Title Case, italicized; Volume: italicized, no label
  • Issue: not italicized, in parentheses immediately after volume
  • No pp. prefix for page ranges in journals

Books

Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of book* (Xth ed.). Publisher.
  • Title: italicized, sentence case; no publication city

Edited Book Chapters

Author, A. A. (Year). Chapter title. In A. Editor & B. Editor (Eds.), *Book Title* (pp. XX–XX). Publisher.
  • Use (Ed.) for one editor, (Eds.) for multiple
  • Chapter title: sentence case, no italics; Book title: italicized

Dissertations & Theses

Surname, A. (Year). *Title* [Unpublished doctoral dissertation]. University.
  • Use doctoral dissertation (not "PhD thesis"); master's thesis (lowercase)
  • No publisher or city needed

Conference Proceedings

Author, A. (Year). Paper title. In A. Editor (Ed.), *Proceedings Title* (pp. XX–XX). Publisher.
  • If no editors: In *Proceedings of the Conference Name* (pp. XX–XX).

Reports, Preprints & Web Sources

// Institutional / Government Report Organization Name [Abbr]. (Year). *Title of report*. https://… // Preprint (arXiv, PsyArXiv, etc.) Author, A. (Year). *Title*. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/XXXX.XXXXX // Web — no DOI available Include URL. Remove "Retrieved from" for stable URLs.
  • Use Author. only if the organization is both author and publisher

Turkish Title Translation

Rule — applies to every Turkish-language title

  1. Append English translation in square brackets immediately after the original title
  2. Do not italicize the bracketed translation for non-book titles (articles, chapters)
  3. Do italicize the translation when it sits inside an already-italicized block (book/thesis title)

Article — before

Çocuklarda zihin kuramının gelişimi. Journal of Edu, 5(2), 10–20.

Article — after

Çocuklarda zihin kuramının gelişimi [Development of theory of mind]. *Journal of Edu*, *5*(2), 10–20.

Book — before

Bilimsel araştırma yöntemi (25th ed.). Ankara: Nobel Publishing.

Book — after

*Bilimsel araştırma yöntemi* [*Scientific research method*] (25th ed.). Nobel Publishing.

Thesis — before

Öğretmenlerin istatistik bilgisi [Unpublished master's thesis]. Trabzon University.

Thesis — after

*Öğretmenlerin istatistik bilgisi* [*Teachers' knowledge of statistics*] [Unpublished master's thesis]. Trabzon University.

Error Detection & Flags

Flag suspicious entries inline. Include a summary in the notes block at the top of each response.

IssueInline Flag
DOI year ≠ publication year[DOI yılı doğrulanmalı]
Impossible page range (e.g. 212–132)[sayfa aralığı doğrulanmalı]
et al. used in reference listAll authors must be listed explicitly
Missing journal or book name[dergi/kitap adı eksik]
Likely typo in author nameCorrect + [doğrulanmalı]
Dual publication years (e.g. 1995, 2006)[tarih doğrulanmalı]
[In press] entriesKeep as-is; complete when published
Missing page numbers for book chapter[sayfa numarası tamamlanmalı]

Output Format

1
Notes block at top — bulleted summary of all changes made and errors detected
2
Formatted reference list — alphabetical by first author surname, hanging indent in markdown
3
Inline flags on suspicious entries — [doğrulanmalı] or [tamamlanmalı]. No commentary between entries; keep the list clean.

Worked Examples

Journal Article

Before

Ball, D. L., Thames, M. H., & Phelps, G. (2008). Content knowledge for teaching: What makes it special? Journal of Teacher Education, 59(5), 389–407.

After

Ball, D. L., Thames, M. H., & Phelps, G. (2008). Content knowledge for teaching: What makes it special? *Journal of Teacher Education*, *59*(5), 389–407.

Turkish Book with City

Before

Karasar, N. (2019). Bilimsel araştırma yöntemi (25th ed.). Ankara: Nobel Publishing.

After

Karasar, N. (2019). *Bilimsel araştırma yöntemi* [*Scientific research method*] (25th ed.). Nobel Publishing.

Edited Chapter

Before

Perner, J. (1999). Theory of mind. In M. Bennett (Ed.), Developmental psychology. Psychology Press.

After

Perner, J. (1999). Theory of mind. In M. Bennett (Ed.), *Developmental psychology*. Psychology Press.
